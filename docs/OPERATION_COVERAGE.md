## What the counts mean

The source universe is every operation in the published SDK inventory [catalog/aws-sdk-operations.json](../catalog/aws-sdk-operations.json): **20537 operations across 440 crates**. An operation is *exercised* when an empty-argument `.send()` callsite resolved to that crate and module at the `Cargo.lock`-pinned version is proven by the live source scan; a scan that cannot resolve the current source makes the count unavailable instead of falling back to reviewed claims. Recorded emulator evidence must not classify an operation as a decode failure. The join is on `(crate, version, module)`, never on a module name alone; the same method name in two services remains two operations.

**This document does not claim that all operations are invoked. 76 of 20537 (0.37%) are exercised; 20461 are not.** An unexercised operation is unimplemented by this collector, not necessarily unsupported by AWS or the SDK.

Cloud Control `ListResources` and `GetResource` are **provider-generic**: they count **once each**, not once per configured resource type. The provider-generic portion is 2 exercised operation(s); typed native collector coverage is 74 operation(s). Descriptor breadth (for example the 418 executable resource types) is resource-type coverage, never operation coverage.

A further 10 reviewed additive operation(s) are listed separately below; they are not exercised and carry no emulator outcome.
